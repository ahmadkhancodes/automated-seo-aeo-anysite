A 40–80 word paragraph that answers the question directly, names the main entity, and mentions the year if the post is a year edition. No warm-up.

## What is [the main entity], and who is it for?

Define the entity in one sentence, then say who benefits and who does not.

## 1. First item

What it is, the evidence (with a link in the same paragraph), and when it fits. **Choose it if** … **Skip it if** …

## 2. Second item

Same structure.

## 3. Third item

Same structure.

## 4. Fourth item

Same structure.

## 5. Fifth item

Same structure.

## What changes in 2027?

Forward-looking claims: each one either comes from a source you opened (linked and dated) or is labelled "our view" and written as reasoning, never as fact.

## What would a 30-day pilot deliver?

Concrete deliverables, what the client provides, how success is measured.

## Is this right for you?

- Three to five signs that the reader is a good fit.

Closing sentence: recommend one concrete next step for this specific offer and say why now. No fake urgency.
